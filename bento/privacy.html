Privacy Policy

Bento+ · Effective August 8, 2026

Bento+ builds a weekly meal plan around your grocery budget and the sales at stores near you. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we need it, and what we never do with it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Bento+ is in public testing. The app is not finished. Features may change or be removed, and data you enter during the test period may be reset. Please do not rely on Bento+ as your only record of anything.

What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to build your plan. There is no advertising network, no analytics SDK, and no third-party tracker in Bento+.

  • Your ZIP code. Used to find grocery stores near you and to pull the correct weekly sale ads. We store the ZIP code you enter, not your precise location, and Bento+ does not request GPS access.
  • The stores you select. Which nearby stores you want the plan built around.
  • Your plan settings. Servings needed per day and the budget you set for the week.
  • Your allergen selections. The allergens you ask Bento+ to exclude. See the section below, because we treat this differently from everything else.
  • Your meal ratings. Which meals you have rated up or down, so the rotation reflects what you actually want to eat.
  • Your generated plans and shopping lists. The weekly plans and lists Bento+ produces for you.
  • Your account. Your email address and any display name you choose. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.
  • Your household. Which household you belong to and who else is in it, so plans and lists stay in sync across everyone's devices.
  • Crash reports. If the app hits an error, it records the error message, a stack trace, the app version and your device platform so the problem can be fixed.

Your allergen list

An allergen list says something about your health, so we hold it to a higher standard than the rest of your settings.

  • It is used for exactly one purpose: removing meals containing those ingredients from your rotation.
  • It is never sold, never shared with retailers or advertisers, and never used to build a profile of you.
  • If you are in a household, the allergen list applied to the household plan is visible to the other members of that household. Nobody outside it can see it.
  • It is not shared with the third parties that provide our recipe, nutrition or pricing data.
  • You can change or clear it at any time in the app, and clearing it removes it from our database.

Please also read the allergen safety section of the Terms of Use. Allergen filtering is a convenience feature built on ingredient data that can be incomplete or wrong, and it is not a substitute for reading the label on the food you actually buy.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location. Bento+ works from the ZIP code you type in, and does not ask for GPS access.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or data from other apps.
  • We do not collect payment details. Bento+ does not process payments and never sees a card number.
  • We do not build advertising profiles, and we have never sold or shared personal data with a data broker.

Households and what your household can see

Bento+ is built for a household rather than a single phone. You can add the people you live with, and the plan, the shopping list and the settings behind them then sync across everyone's devices.

This is worth understanding before you add anyone, because it is the one place your information is visible to another person:

  • Everyone in your household can see the household's meal plans and shopping lists.
  • Everyone in your household can see the settings that shape those plans, including the ZIP code, the selected stores, the budget, the servings, and the allergen list applied to the household plan.
  • Anyone in the household can change those settings, which changes the plan for everybody.
  • You choose who joins. Only add people you would be comfortable sharing all of the above with.
  • You can leave a household at any time, and the person who created it can remove members.

Nothing is shared outside your household. Bento+ has no public feed, no social features, and no way for a stranger to find you.

How your data is used

Your settings, ratings, plans and lists are used to generate and improve the meal plans for you and your household. They are not published, shown to anyone outside your household, or posted anywhere public.

We may look at aggregate, de-identified information, for example how often plans come in under budget, to improve how the planner works. This never includes your allergen list and is never tied back to you.

Where your data lives

Account data, settings, ratings and generated plans are stored with Supabase, our hosting and database provider, on servers in the United States. Access is enforced by row-level security rules so that one account cannot read another account's data, and so that household data is readable only by members of that household.

The app also requests public data from third parties in order to build your plan. These requests include the ZIP code or the stores you selected so the right prices come back, but never your identity, your account, or your allergen list:

  • Weekly grocery sale ad and pricing data for the stores in your area.
  • Recipe, ingredient and nutrition information for the meal library.

Store names and trademarks

Bento+ shows the names of grocery stores so you can choose where to shop. Those names and logos belong to their owners. Bento+ is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any retailer, and no retailer has any involvement in the plans the app produces.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your ZIP code, stores, budget, servings, allergens and ratings at any time in the app.
  • You can delete your account, which permanently removes your profile, settings, allergen list, ratings, plans and shopping lists. This cannot be undone.
  • You can leave your household at any time, and the person who created it can remove members.
  • You can request a copy of your data by emailing us.
  • If you are in a jurisdiction with additional rights over your personal data, such as the right to correct or restrict processing, email us and we will honor the request.

Children

Bento+ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed from our live database immediately, and routine encrypted backups are cycled out within 30 days. Crash reports are kept for up to 12 months.

Because Bento+ is in public testing, we may reset test data between builds. We will give notice in the app before doing so where we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or privacy concerns: email us. We reply within a few days.
