Stringer Pro

Pre-launch

The Illinois Angler

Everything an Illinois angler needs, in one app. 441 public lakes and rivers with ramps, piers and access points, over 400 lake maps showing depth and structure, live stocking alerts, river gauges and a seven day bite forecast. Log a catch and the app records the conditions around it, so your own patterns show up over a season.

Inspiration for building

Fishing is my number one hobby, and I was tired of paying high fees for apps that still missed what I actually needed. I wanted live weather, bite windows, stocking reports, and species and bait guidance in one place, easy to share with friends, with my spots kept private.

Bento+

Building

Meal planning · Budget aware

An answer to the question nobody in the house wants to ask: what is for dinner? You put in a ZIP code, pick the stores you actually shop at, and set how many servings you need a day along with what you want to spend for the week. Bento+ builds the plan around what is on sale near you, then gives you the shopping list to go with it.

  • Plans to a budget, not just a tasteServings and a weekly spend go in, a costed plan comes out. Most meal planners ignore price completely.
  • Built on this week's local salesWeekly ads come from your ZIP code and get matched against the stores you picked, so the plan follows what is actually cheap right now.
  • Learns what you will not eat250 meals on a rotating schedule. Thumb one down and it is gone for good. Set your allergens once and anything containing them drops out permanently.
  • Everything you need to cook itNutrition and prep directions on every meal, plus a shopping list grouped by store aisle with every item tagged to the meal it belongs to.
  • Built for a household, not one phoneAdd the people you live with and the plan, the list and the settings behind them stay in sync across everyone's devices.
ZIP-based pricing Weekly sales ads Allergen filtering Nutrition data Auto shopping list Household sync

Inspiration for building

My fiancé and my mother both live with autoimmune disease, and building a rotating meal plan that was budget friendly and safe for them was a nightmare. Bento+ does it in thirty seconds. A ZIP code, a budget, the servings you need, and all that is left is the grocery list.

RoboWash

Early build

Autonomous laundry handling

A machine that takes laundry off the pile and gives it back folded. RoboWash is a joint project, and I lead the software: the sequencing that moves a garment through intake, separation, folding and stacking, which is the part of the problem that is not mechanical. Every line written so far is mine. It runs the full work cycle in simulation today, with hardware to follow, and the software stays closed.

Inspiration for building

Laundry is one of the most time consuming and least enjoyable chores in any household, everywhere. RoboWash is our attempt to take it off people's hands completely and give them back the hours it quietly costs them every week. I am building the control software first.

How I work

Design, build, ship

Whole products, not pieces

Interface, backend, data pipeline, store listing, privacy policy. Stringer Pro went from an idea to a finished, compliant build with no handoff, because there was nobody to hand it to.

Narrow beats broad

Stringer Pro serves Illinois anglers, not anglers. That is what leaves room for 441 named waters, stocking reports and site-specific limits a national app would never carry.

Privacy is a feature

No ad networks, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, and a privacy policy written to be read instead of survived. Access rules are enforced in the database, not just the interface.

Software and the machine

When a product needs hardware, I build the hardware. The laundry robot comes from the same instinct as the apps, pointed at a physical problem.
